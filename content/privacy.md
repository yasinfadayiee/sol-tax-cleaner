# Privacy

Last updated: 22 October 2026.

- **Everything runs in your browser.** The addresses you enter, the transactions read and the files produced stay on your device.
- **Network requests.** Your browser talks only to the Solana RPC endpoint you use (by default the public Solana endpoint, or the one you enter) to read public blockchain data. That provider sees your IP address and the addresses you query, under its own privacy policy.
- **Storage.** Fetched transactions, token names, your RPC setting and your unlock status are stored in your browser (IndexedDB and local storage) so repeat runs are fast. Clear your site data to remove them.
- **No cookies, no analytics, no accounts.** We do not collect emails or personal data.
- **Payments** are ordinary public Solana transactions between your wallet and ours.
